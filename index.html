Core competencies

Agent chains that don't degrade

Long agent chains drift and compound errors. I engineer that out: a quality gate at every hand-off (its own pull request, ~4,000 backend tests, cross-review, QA acceptance), human-in-the-loop only for releases, merges, secrets and external replies, every decision in a register, and any agent restartable from its knowledge base in about 10 minutes to reset drift. Post-mortems and a risk register close the loop. Live with 7 agents; designed to scale to 10+.
